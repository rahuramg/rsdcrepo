Welcome to Tooth Defender: Dental Dash! Learn how to play, understand all game objects, and discover real-world dental health benefits.

5 Game Levels

Level 1: Sugar Bug Rampage (0 - 400m) – Jump over crawling sugar monsters and collect toothbrushes!
Level 2: Cavity & Decay Attack (400 - 900m) – Bouncing cavity blobs appear; collect Toothpaste for Fluoride Shields!
Level 3: Bacteria Swarm Sky (900 - 1500m) – Flying microbes appear; use Duck/Slide or Jump-Zap!

Level 5: Master Fluoride Kingdom (2200m+) – Ultimate speed & obstacles with score multipliers!

Obstacles & Germ Enemies

👾

Sugar Bug (Ground Crawling)

Game Danger: Causes -1 Heart damage. Jump over or Zap!
Dental Danger: Sugar feeds oral bacteria which produce destructive acid that dissolves enamel.
🦠

Decay Blob (Bouncing Cavity)

Game Danger: Causes -1 Heart damage.
Dental Danger: Untreated tooth decay causes painful cavities and deep root infections.
😈

Bacteria Swarm (Flying Microbe)

Game Danger: Flies in mid-air. Duck/Slide under or Zap!
Dental Danger: Plaque bacteria produce toxins that inflame gums and cause gingivitis.
🧪

Soda Acid Puddle (Ground Pool)

Game Danger: Floor hazard. Must double-jump over!
Dental Danger: Acidic sodas and energy drinks strip away protective enamel layers directly.
